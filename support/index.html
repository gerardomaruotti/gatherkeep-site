Gatherkeep support

Gatherkeep is an iPhone and iPad app for links, notes, photos, files, and voice notes. It isn’t on the App Store yet, and this page covers the beta interest list as well as the app.

Contact

Questions, feedback, or something not working? Email Gerardo Maruotti. If you’re reporting a problem, please say which device and which iOS or iPadOS version you use, and what happened.

Email maruotti@icloud.com

Frequently asked questions

Is Gatherkeep available to download?

Not yet. Gatherkeep isn’t on the App Store. You can join the beta interest list from the home page; we’ll email you if a beta place opens, and access isn’t guaranteed.

What can I save?

Links, notes, photos, PDFs and other files, and voice notes. You can find them again with search, tags, and type filters.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad. Mac is coming soon, with no release date yet.

Can I use it offline?

Saved items work offline; webpages need a connection. Transcription depends on your device, language, and downloaded assets.

What happens to my data?

Items stay on your device, with optional iCloud sync. There is no Gatherkeep account and no analytics. Supported processing, such as text recognition and voice-note transcription, happens on your device.

How do I remove my email from the beta list?

Email maruotti@icloud.com from the address you signed up with and ask for it to be deleted. You can also ask to see or correct your details.
