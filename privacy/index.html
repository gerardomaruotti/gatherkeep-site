Privacy

Gerardo Maruotti manages the Gatherkeep beta interest list. You can contact him at maruotti@icloud.com.

What we collect and why

If you submit the form, we collect your email address and chosen page language so we can contact you about an opportunity to try the Gatherkeep beta. Signing up does not guarantee an invitation. We do not use this list for a newsletter or unrelated marketing.

Where it goes

Formspree receives and stores the form submission and sends a notification to the Gatherkeep contact email. GitHub Pages serves this website; GitHub’s privacy statement explains its handling of site visits. This site does not add analytics.

How long we keep it

We keep beta interest details while recruiting testers and delete them when they are no longer needed. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your details at any time by emailing maruotti@icloud.com.
