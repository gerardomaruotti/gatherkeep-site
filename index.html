Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What can I save?

Links, notes, photos, PDFs and other files, and voice notes. Capture options include sharing, clipboard, camera, and document scanning.

Which devices are supported?

Gatherkeep is built for iPhone and iPad. A Mac version is coming later; availability is not announced yet.

Can I use it offline?

Saved items remain available locally. Opening webpages needs a connection. Voice transcription depends on device, language, and downloaded assets.

What happens to my data?

Items are saved locally, with optional iCloud sync. Gatherkeep has no separate account or analytics; supported processing happens on device.
